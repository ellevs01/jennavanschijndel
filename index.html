Bio

"I love connecting with audiences through singing, acting, and storytelling."

Jenna Van Schijndel is a young performer from Orange County, California, with a passion for singing, acting, and storytelling.

Jenna currently attends the Orange County School of the Arts (OCSA), where she is a student in the Acting Conservatory. She also trains with Next Big Thing Academy and is represented by AE Talent.
